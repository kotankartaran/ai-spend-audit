# Unit Economics

## Problem

AI software costs are increasing rapidly for startups and engineering teams.

Organizations often:
- overpay for enterprise plans
- maintain inactive seats
- duplicate subscriptions across tools

---

## Value Proposition

AI Spend Audit helps companies:
- reduce recurring AI expenses
- identify unnecessary plans
- improve subscription allocation

---

## Potential Revenue Model

### Free Tier
- limited audits
- basic recommendations

### Pro Tier
- unlimited audits
- AI-generated optimization reports
- team analytics
- exportable reports

### Enterprise Tier
- centralized spend tracking
- SSO
- procurement integrations
- advanced reporting

---

## Cost Structure

Main infrastructure costs:
- Vercel hosting
- AI API usage
- database hosting
- authentication services

---

## Expansion Opportunities

- procurement automation
- SaaS optimization platform
- AI usage governance
- subscription benchmarking
