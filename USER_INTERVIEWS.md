# User Research Notes

## Target Users

- Startup founders
- Engineering managers
- Product teams
- AI-heavy startups

## Common Pain Points

### 1. Subscription Sprawl

Teams often subscribe to multiple overlapping AI products.

### 2. Unused Seats

Many companies continue paying for inactive users.

### 3. Poor Visibility

Organizations lack centralized AI spend tracking.

### 4. Over-Provisioned Plans

Teams frequently upgrade to enterprise tiers unnecessarily.

## Insights

Users preferred:
- fast audits
- simple dashboards
- shareable reports
- savings visibility
